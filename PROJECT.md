# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openrecommend

**Short description:** Learn how apps guess what you will like: similarity, prediction, matrix factorisation and fair testing, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openrecommend is a beginner-friendly learning repo that teaches how a recommender system works. In three short sessions you go from a tiny ratings table of 5 people and 6 films that you solve by hand, to cosine and Pearson similarity with user-based and item-based prediction tested on hidden ratings, to matrix factorisation by alternating least squares, where you pick the number of hidden factors on validation data, see overfitting and data leakage, measure the cold-start problem for a brand-new user, and measure how leaning on popularity shrinks the variety of what is shown. All data is synthetic and made up for teaching. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as bias terms, smarter cold start, diversity-aware ranking and closed-loop simulations. It is education only; real systems must also respect privacy and fairness.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
