openrecommend

Build a recommender that suggests what you might like, in three sessions.

  • Session 1: People who agree keep agreeing (Level 1, 60 min)
  • Session 2: Measure similarity, predict, and test honestly (Level 2, 75 min)
  • Session 3: Hidden tastes, cold start and shrinking variety (Level 3, 90 min)
